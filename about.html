SENCHOU is an app for the local amateur E-ATHLETES of the world. It allows you to score your local Esports match. It also allows you to add your own team, search players, search teams, find tournaments around you, analyse your performance, share your achievements and much more. In one line, SENCHOU is your new GAMING Network!

Just like you, we are local amateur GAMERS and used to discuss and fight over our performances in our daily scrims and tournaments. In gaming, stats are everything. Without stats, most discussions are empty talks. When we thought over it more, we realised that this is a pain point for almost every gamer like ourselves. That became the start of what is known as SENCHOU app today.

If you want to preserve your daily and tournament performances forever, analyse your stats to improve, find local teams, ORGs or Players, and get recognition for your talent - Senchou is for you.

Tap the button "Register my Team" from the side menu. You just need to give your team name and team logo (optional) to create your own team on SENCHOU app. Immediately after that, you can start adding players to your team. The team name has to be unique so book your team name now before it's too late.

Only those who have registered as ORG can score a match. Note: No player can register as an ORG.

To score a match, go to the sidebar and select "Start a match", then select teams and start scoring. Note: Teams only registered will show up in results, so make sure to register in advance. We are constantly improving this workflow, but it will always be pretty much self-explanatory.
