Manifesto

Why we built Senchou

Who really played better in last night’s scrim?

Every gamer has had that argument. We had it after almost every match. Just like you, we’re local amateur gamers, and our daily scrims and tournaments always ended the same way: a passionate debate about who carried, who choked and who deserved the win.

In gaming, stats are everything. Without them, most of those debates are just talk. People remember a lot of stats, but nobody can remember them all, and the scoreboard is gone the moment the next match starts.

When we thought about it more, we realised this wasn’t just our problem. It was a pain point for almost every amateur player, team and organiser out there. Pros have analysts and broadcasts. The rest of us had screenshots and group chats.

So we built Senchou. Organisers score matches live. Every result rolls into standings, team records and player profiles, and stays there for good. Players and organisers each get their own space, because they need different things from a match.

Most of all, we built it for the amateurs and the underdogs. Senchou is a way for players who’ve never had a stage to reach pro level and make everyone know their name.

Today, players from 150 countries keep score on Senchou, and we’re only getting started.

We’ll keep the score. You just play.

— Aakash and Ishaan

Aakash Dhupar, Founder · Ishaan Bajaj, Co-Founder & Developer
