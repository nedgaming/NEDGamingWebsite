About

Esports and gaming have rapidly grown in popularity, attracting millions of players and spectators worldwide. However, the lack of effective scorekeeping and statistical analysis tools has been a major challenge for both players and organizers. The Senchou App fills this gap by providing a comprehensive platform that enhances the gaming experience for all stakeholders. With its unique separate registration for players and organisers, the Senchou App has truly revolutionized the world of esports and gaming, bringing advanced scorekeeping and statistics to players and enthusiasts alike. Its a great tool for amateurs and underdogs players to reach the level of PRO and make everyone know their name. .

SENCHOU is an app for the local amateur E-ATHLETES of the world. It allows you to score your local Esports match . It also allows you to add your own team, search players, search teams, find tournaments around you, analyse your performance, share your achievements and much more… In one line, SENCHOU is your new GAMING Network!

Just like you, We are local amateur GAMERS and used to discuss and fight over our performances in our daily scrims and tourni's . The discussions inevitably became very passionate. In Gaming stats are everything. Without stats, most of the discussions are empty talks. Amazingly people do remember many stats but they possibly can’t remember it all. When we thought over it more, we realised that this is a pain point for almost every gamer like ourselves. That became a start of what is known as SENCHOU app today.

If you want to preserve your daily and tournnaments performances forever, analyse your stats to improve, find local teams, ORGs or Players , get recognition for your talent.

Tap the button “Register my Team” from the side menu. You just need to give your team name, team logo (optional) to create your own team on SENCHOU app Immediately after that, you can start adding players to your team. The team name has to be unique so book your team name now before its too late.

Only those who have registered as ORG can score a match. NOTE-NO PLAYER CAN REGISTER AS AN ORG

TO score a match go to side bar and select start a match select teams and start scoring. NOTE- TEAMS ONLY REGISTERED WILL SHOW UP IN RESULT SO MAKE SURE TO REGISTER IN ADVANCE. We are constantly improving this workflow so things may change here and there but it will always be pretty much self-explanatory.
