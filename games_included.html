"Genres And Games"

Video gaming has a wide variety of genres or "types", and so does competitive gaming - ESPORTS.
Some of the most popular games among professional competitors are:



TACTICAL SHOOTERS

First Person perspective games where players compete as a team to secure objectives and defeat the rival team.
For example:

Counter Strike

PUBG



MOBA GAMES

Strategic games where players compete as a team to destroy the opposing team's main structure.
For example:

League Of Legends

DOTA



SPORTS SIMULATORS

Virtual simulation games of existing sports such as Football, Basketball, Cycling and others.
For example:

FIFA

NBA



FIGHTING GAMES

Close combat games where two or more players compete and utlize skills to defeat their opponent.
For example:

Street Fighter

Mortal Kombat

Information Source: IEF a.k.a International ESports Federation
