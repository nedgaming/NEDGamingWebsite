Genres And Games

Video gaming has a wide variety of genres or "types", and so does competitive gaming - ESPORTS. Some of the most popular games among professional competitors are:

Tactical Shooters

First Person perspective games where players compete as a team to secure objectives and defeat the rival team.

Counter Strike
PUBG

MOBA Games

Strategic games where players compete as a team to destroy the opposing team's main structure.

League Of Legends
DOTA

Sports Simulators

Virtual simulation games of existing sports such as Football, Basketball, Cycling and others.

FIFA
NBA

Fighting Games

Close combat games where two or more players compete and utilize skills to defeat their opponent.

Street Fighter
Mortal Kombat

Information Source: IEF a.k.a International ESports Federation
