Delete your account

We’re sorry to see you go. Follow the steps below to permanently delete your Senchou account.

Before you delete

Deleting your account will permanently remove all your data, including:

  • Your profile and statistics
  • Team memberships and history
  • Match records and achievements

This action cannot be undone.

Follow these steps

  1. 1

    Open My Account

    Go to the My Account tab in the side navigation panel of the app.

  2. 2

    Find the delete option

    Scroll down and tap Delete Account Permanently at the bottom of the screen.

  3. 3

    Confirm deletion

    Tap Yes to confirm. You’ll be logged out and your account will be permanently deleted.

Need help?

If you’re having trouble deleting your account or have any questions, email us at ned.esports@gmail.com or visit the contact page. You can also read how we handle your data in our Privacy Policy.
