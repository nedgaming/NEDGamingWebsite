Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go. Follow the steps below to permanently delete your Senchou account.

Before You Delete

Deleting your account will permanently remove all your data, including:

  • Your profile and statistics
  • Team memberships and history
  • Match records and achievements

This action cannot be undone.

How To Delete

Follow These Steps

1

Open My Account

Go to the My Account tab in the Side Navigation panel of the app.

2

Find Delete Option

Scroll down and click on the Delete Account Permanently option at the bottom of the screen.

3

Confirm Deletion

Click on Yes to confirm your account deletion. After this, you will be logged out and your account will be permanently deleted.

Need Help?

If you're having trouble deleting your account or have any questions, feel free to contact us.
